*Buenos Aires On Film* is a 12-page A5 photo zine featuring photographs captured on a Nikon F70 using Harman Phoenix color film. 

This publication explores the unique nature of Buenos Aires's wall art culture, capturing the vibrant street murals that serve as canvas for public expression. Through the distinct grain and color characteristics of Harman Phoenix film, the photographs trace the contrasts of these artistic expressions against the backdrop of a city undergoing rapid transformations, gentrification, and modernisation.
