*On The Go* is an 8-page A7 mini photo zine displaying analog photographs coupled with reflective texts discussing public transport options and the artist's personal thoughts on each of them.

From local trains and buses to airports and planes, this publication documents transit spaces and the unique, liminal moments of waiting and traveling. Combining grainy film exposures with brief, candid commentary, *On The Go* offers a quick but engaging exploration of daily commutes and long journeys alike.
