A striking, high-key exploration of architectural form and light. The city skyline is enveloped in a brilliant, almost blinding winter atmosphere, casting the skyscrapers as delicate, frozen pillars between the high-exposure sky and the calm, mirrored surface of the lake. 

The composition emphasizes structural geometry and high-contrast minimalism, stripping the busy metropolis down to its bare, luminous reflections.
